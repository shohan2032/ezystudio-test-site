Once a season we push every table in the room together and cook one menu for everyone.

## The evening

Doors at 6.30pm, sitting down at 7. Five courses built around the last of the autumn produce — expect the wood oven to be doing most of the work — each with a cider chosen with the orchard that makes it.

Vegetarian? Tell us when you book and you'll get your own version of every course.

## Tickets

£65 a head, including the cider pairing. Forty seats, sold by phone on **0117 496 0123** or by [sending us an enquiry](/events/#enquire). Last time they went in a week.
