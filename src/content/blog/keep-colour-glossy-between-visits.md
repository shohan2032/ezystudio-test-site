Colour fades for three reasons: water, heat and sun. You cannot avoid any of them completely, but
you can slow all three down. These are the habits I ask every colour client to try.

## 1. Wait 48 hours before the first wash

The cuticle needs a couple of days to close fully around new colour. Wash too soon and a little
of it rinses straight down the drain.

## 2. Turn the water down

Hot water opens the cuticle; colour slips out. Wash in warm water and finish with a cool rinse.
It feels bracing in January, but you will see the difference in shine.

## 3. Use a sulphate-free shampoo, and less of it

A coin-sized amount, worked into the scalp only, is plenty. The suds that run down the lengths
clean them without stripping the ends.

## 4. Protect from heat

Use a heat protectant every time you blow-dry, straighten or curl, and keep tools below 185°C
(365°F). Blondes in particular turn brassy under high heat.

## 5. Book a gloss halfway

A 45-minute gloss between full colour appointments puts back tone and shine, and costs far less
than a full colour. Most clients come in for one around week six.
