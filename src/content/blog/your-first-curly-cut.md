If you have spent years having your curls cut wet and pulled straight, a dry curly cut can feel
strange the first time. Here is what happens, step by step.

## Before you come in

Arrive with your hair dry, clean and styled the way you usually wear it, with no buns, braids or
clips. Day two hair is perfect. I want to see how your curls behave on an ordinary day.

## The consultation

We start sitting down. I will ask how you style your hair, what bothers you about it and what you
love. Bring photos if you have them, of your own hair on a good day as well as inspiration.

## The cut

I cut each curl where it sits, one at a time. Because I can see exactly where every curl lands, there
are no surprises when it dries: no shrinkage shock, no triangle shape.

## Wash, style and a routine

After the cut I wash, condition and style your hair, and talk you through each step so you can do
it at home in fifteen minutes. You leave knowing which products to use, and how much.

## How often to come back

Most curls need a cut every three to four months. If you are growing out a perm, relaxer or heat
damage, every eight to ten weeks moves things along faster.
